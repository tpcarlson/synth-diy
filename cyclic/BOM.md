For full kits the SMT parts will already be soldered and are omitted.

##### Customisation

Should you wish to customise your Cyclic:

- Skip the tactile buttons if you're very confident in your programming ability!

- 6 of the heat-set inserts and screws are used to secure the case together, with the other 4 used to secure the button pad PCB to the bottom case. There are holes for up to 16 heat set inserts and screws for the PCB/case connection but you should not need them

- Do not use a power supply with more than 5v output. Cyclic is designed to run from USB-C drawing up to 2A, or an external supply of at least 1A

- Use whatever colour you want for the top and bottom halves of the case
